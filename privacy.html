Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Haven House Co. sells ex-UK furniture and home decor in Nairobi, Kenya. This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, checkout or contact forms, and it does not ask you for personal details. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

When you tap Ask on WhatsApp, WhatsApp opens with a message that names the item, its category and condition, and a link to its photo. If you send it, we receive your WhatsApp number, your name as it appears there, and whatever you write. We use this only to reply about the item, arrange payment and delivery, and keep a record of the sale.

Services that process data for us

  • WhatsApp (Meta) delivers your messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Supabase stores and serves our product listings and photos.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. We share it only with a delivery provider when needed to deliver your order, or when the law requires it. We keep WhatsApp conversations for as long as needed to complete a sale and handle any later question about it.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Message us on WhatsApp or call +254 785 236 144 to make a request. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
